### Question：Does the Portrayal of Love Between a Man and a Woman in the Song of Songs Help Us See God’s Love?

{% include BibleLinks2026.html %}

### Meditation: God's Story   
+ The bridegroom praises his beloved, saying she is beautiful as Tirzah, lovely as Jerusalem, and majestic as an army with banners.
+ The bride invites her beloved to go with her to the countryside, stay in the villages, and see whether the vines have blossomed.
+ The bride loves her beloved so deeply that she wishes he were like her own brother, so they could be close to each other without restraint.
+ “Place me like a seal over your heart, like a seal on your arm.”
+ “Love is as strong as death, and jealousy as unyielding as the grave. Its flashes are flashes of fire, a most vehement flame—the very flame of the LORD.”

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Identity** - The faithful love portrayed in the Song of Songs is the love between a man and a woman. It also reminds us that God’s love for us is so deeply passionate and unforgettable—indeed, even greater than this. Reflect on God’s love for us, and respond to Him in prayer.
