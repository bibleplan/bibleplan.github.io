### Question：How Does the Bridegroom Describe His Beloved?

{% include BibleLinks2026.html %}

### Meditation: God's Story   
+ The descriptions of King Solomon’s wealth, power, and prosperity seem to stand in contrast to the purity of romantic love.
+ The bridegroom praises his bride, calling her his beloved and declaring that she is exceedingly beautiful!
+ The bridegroom calls the woman his sister and compares her to a garden locked up, a spring enclosed, and a fountain sealed, with all kinds of choice fruits growing in the garden.
+ In her sleep, the bride hears her beloved’s voice once again, as though she is lovesick with longing for him.
+ The bride praises the bridegroom, describing him as her beloved, radiant and ruddy, outstanding among ten thousand.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Community** - In the Song of Songs, the descriptions exchanged by the man and woman in love express their deep affection and longing for each other. Do we regularly express our love for our spouses through both words and actions?
