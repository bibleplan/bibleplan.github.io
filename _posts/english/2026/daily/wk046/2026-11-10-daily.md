### Question：Why Did the LORD’s Response Lead Job to Reproach Himself?

{% include BibleLinks2026.html %}

### Meditation: God's Story   
+ The LORD challenges Job not to annul His purposes and reveals that His almighty power is beyond human comprehension.
+ Job confesses his sin and reproaches himself. Before, he had heard of God by hearsay; now, he has seen God with his own eyes. Therefore, he repents in dust and ashes.
+ The LORD’s anger burns against Job’s three friends because they have not spoken rightly about Him, as Job has.
+ Job prays for his friends, and the LORD restores his fortunes and gives him twice as much as he had before.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Identity** - God does not explain the reason for Job’s suffering. Instead, He reveals His almighty power and contrasts it with human frailty. What does this stir in me as I consider my position as a created being before the Creator? How does this help me face the suffering I experience?
+ **Community** - Job’s friends’ words failed to comfort him and instead exposed their own shortcomings. Why?
+ **Identity** - What wisdom do we see in the Book of Job?
