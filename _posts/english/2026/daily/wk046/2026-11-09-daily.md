### Meditation: God's Story   
+ The LORD begins by asking a fundamental question about wisdom: “Who is this that obscures my plans with words without knowledge?”
+ What follows is a series of questions about creation, intended to give Job a new perspective: Where were you when all these things were created?
+ Finally, Job responds with humility and silence.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Identity** - What do we feel when we recognize that God is the Creator of the world? How does this change our understanding of ourselves? How does it change our understanding of the world?
