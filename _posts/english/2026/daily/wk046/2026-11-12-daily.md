### Question：Why Does the Teacher Continue to Seek Wisdom Amid Vanity?

{% include BibleLinks2026.html %}

### Meditation: God's Story   
+ Guard your steps when you go to the house of God. Do not be hasty in speaking before Him.
+ When people eat, drink, and enjoy the days of the life God has given them, this is God’s gift.
+ When God grants someone wealth, riches, and honor, these can prove to be vanity and a source of trouble.
+ It is better to listen to the rebuke of the wise than to listen to the songs of fools.
+ The end of a matter is better than its beginning, and patience is better than pride.
+ Wisdom alone can preserve the life of the wise; this is the benefit of knowledge.
+ I said I would become wise, but wisdom remained far from me. The meaning of all things is far beyond me, exceedingly deep. Who can fathom it?
+ Keep the king’s command, and do not stubbornly persist in doing evil.
+ Those who fear God will ultimately fare well, but the wicked will not fare well because they do not fear God.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Identity** - The Teacher encourages us to approach the worship of God in the right way: to guard our steps and not be hasty in speaking before Him. What insights does this offer for our attitude toward worship?



【Character and Spiritual Formation】 The Teacher neither idolizes wisdom nor despises it, but seeks to live in accordance with it. Even though our lives are short, wisdom is still better than foolishness. Where do we seek wisdom?
