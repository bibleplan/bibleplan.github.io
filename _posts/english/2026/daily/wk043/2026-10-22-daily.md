### Meditation: God's Story   
+ This passage sets the scene for the dialogues that follow in the Book of Job.
+ Job, a man from the land of Uz, was blameless and upright. He feared God and shunned evil.
+ Job regularly offered burnt offerings according to the number of his children.
+ Satan claimed that Job feared God because of God's blessings upon him.
+ Job lost his livestock, flocks, camels, and children, yet he did not sin or accuse God of wrongdoing.
+ Satan struck Job again, afflicting him with painful sores from the soles of his feet to the crown of his head. His wife urged him to curse God, but Job did not sin.
+ Job's three friends, Eliphaz, Bildad, and Zophar, mourned for him and came to comfort him.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Fidelity** - The Jews assembled and fought together to turn sorrow into joy. God's protection is in His miraculous plan but we also need to do our part and work together to fend off our enemies. Are we still lacking in any area of our defense? How may I pray for it?
