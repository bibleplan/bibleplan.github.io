### Meditation: God's Story   
+ Bildad rebukes Job for arguing with words and describes the calamities that befall the wicked.
+ Job responds by accusing his friends of troubling his heart and crushing him with their words.
+ Job firmly believes that he will ultimately be delivered. He knows that his Redeemer lives and that in the end He will stand upon the earth.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Identity** - Despite his suffering and his friends’ lack of understanding, Job believes that he will ultimately receive salvation. Does this kind of faith help me? Why or why not?
