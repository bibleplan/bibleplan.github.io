### Question：Why Could Zophar’s Response Not Comfort Job?

{% include BibleLinks2026.html %}

### Meditation: God's Story   
+ Zophar repeats the view that although evildoers may prosper temporarily, they will ultimately perish.
+ Job points out the reality that the wicked can defy God and still enjoy peace and prosperity. He questions his friends’ insistence that God will swiftly punish the wicked.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Identity** - We often see examples of the wicked defying God while enjoying peace and prosperity in this world. How do we feel when we see this? How can these experiences help us gain a deeper understanding of God’s purposes?
+ **Identity** - Zophar insists that God must act immediately to punish evil and reward goodness because he fails to recognize that God is free to act or not to act according to His sovereign will. Do we acknowledge that God is beyond the limits of our understanding? How can recognizing this help us?
