### Question：How Did Eliphaz Rebuke Job?

{% include BibleLinks2026.html %}

### Meditation: God's Story   
+ Eliphaz rebukes Job for speaking arrogantly about his own righteousness, abandoning reverence for God, and undermining a spirit of godliness before Him.
+ Eliphaz describes the suffering of the wicked, saying that they live in constant fear and distress.
+ Job responds by arguing that his friends’ comfort has only increased his anguish and that their consolation has brought him even more sorrow.
+ Job’s grief remains unbearable, and he pours out his lament before God.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Community** - Eliphaz’s attempts to comfort Job gradually turn into rebukes. How can I speak the truth in love to comfort and build others up?
