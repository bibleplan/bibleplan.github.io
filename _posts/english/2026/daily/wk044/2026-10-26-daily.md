### Question：How Did Bildad, Job’s Second Friend, Try to Comfort Job?

{% include BibleLinks2026.html %}

### Meditation: God's Story   
+ Bildad continues along the lines of Eliphaz’s argument, believing that God is just and therefore suffering is punishment for wrongdoing.
+ Bildad relies on traditional teachings and the natural order: God will not reject a blameless person or support evildoers.
+ Job believes that no one can be righteous before God and that his friends’ words have been of no help.
+ Job is anguished by the prospect of presenting his case before God because he is uncertain of the outcome, and he pours out his grief in lament.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Community** - When should we avoid offering comfort in the way Bildad did?
