### Question：Why Was Elihu Angry with Job?

{% include BibleLinks2026.html %}

### Meditation: God's Story   
+ Elihu was angry with Job because Job considered himself righteous. He was also angry with Job’s three other friends.
+ Elihu presents his own perspective. Although he is young, he hopes to persuade Job through honest and sincere words.
+ Elihu believes that Job should not protest his innocence, because God disciplines people in order to save their lives.
+ Elihu rebukes Job for being self-righteous and complaining against God.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Identity** - Elihu acknowledges that righteous people sometimes do suffer, because discipline is intended for their good. How can I recognize God’s goodness and purposes in the midst of suffering?
