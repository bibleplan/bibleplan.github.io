### Meditation: God's Story   
+ Eliphaz categorically lists Job’s alleged sins without allowing room for dispute, and urges him to repent and come to know God so that he may find peace.
+ Job once again expresses his desire to plead his case before God and clear his name. He declares that he has kept the Lord’s ways and has not turned aside from them.
+ Job laments that the righteous and the wicked alike meet the same fate in death.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Identity** - When we feel hopeless and helpless, especially when we are unjustly accused by others, how can we pray and seek the Lord?
