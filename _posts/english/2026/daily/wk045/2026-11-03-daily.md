### Question：Why Were Bildad’s Words Unfair to Job?

{% include BibleLinks2026.html %}

### Meditation: God's Story   
+ Bildad declares that no one can be justified before God.
+ Job acknowledges God’s majesty but ridicules Bildad’s conclusion.

### Meditation: My Story   
+ **Identity** - Through our faith in Jesus, we have been justified before God. How is our situation similar to or different from Job’s?
+ **Identity** - Job’s three friends insist that Job must confess his sin because this is how they understand God to work. What warning does this offer us? Are we limiting God according to our own assumptions, or do we recognize that God is greater than we can comprehend?
